# Ingenium — Outcomes

- **Duolingo-style study dashboard and lesson path** — The app is mobile-friendly, removes unnecessary introductory information, presents Mathematics and Physics in a clear sequence, shows path progress, completed lessons, remaining topics, and quick actions to continue studying or start the mini-test.
- **Complete Mathematics and Physics topic coverage** — Mathematics includes algebra; equations and inequalities; functions and graphs; systems of equations; powers, roots, and logarithms; trigonometry; geometry; vectors; sequences; basic limits and derivatives; probability and statistics; and word problems. Physics includes units and measurement; vectors; motion; Newton's laws; forces and friction; work, energy, and power; momentum and collisions; circular motion; pressure and fluids; heat and temperature; electricity and circuits; magnetism; and basic waves and optics.
- **Priority engineering track** — The interface highlights extra-priority topics for Engenharia Industrial e Sistemas Eléctricos, including algebra and trigonometry, vectors, mechanics, electricity, Ohm's law, series and parallel circuits, power and energy, and units and conversions.
- **Sequential worked solutions** — Concept explanations and practical worked examples reveal each solution sequentially, step by step.
- **Guided practice** — Lessons include exercises with optional hints, answer reveals, and clear step-by-step explanations after an attempt.
- **Persistent progress** — Completion controls and visible completion states remember progress locally in the browser.
- **Timed mini-test** — A 20-minute test spans relevant Mathematics and Physics material, has a visible countdown, and presents exam-style questions.
- **Self-marking and review** — At the end, the app automatically marks the test, shows score feedback, correct answers, and explanatory solution review.
